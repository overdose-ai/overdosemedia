Who we are

A creative ecosystem, not just a service provider

OverDOSE bridges the gap between raw talent and commercial opportunity. Founded by young, visionary entrepreneurs, we deliver premium visual and sound production alongside strategic events — underpinned by one core philosophy: Self Excellence.

Our mission is to empower youth and creatives by providing market access, fostering collaborations, and sharing the industry knowledge that's usually hardest to come by — from remuneration norms to how real partnerships get built.

The vision: to become the leading platform for youth empowerment and creative collaboration in Southern Africa's media landscape.

What we shoot and mix with

Our primary full-frame mirrorless body for music videos, documentaries and event coverage.
